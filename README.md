# Central Legal Pages Repository

This repository contains the hosted legal pages (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Refund Policy) for all mobile apps published under our organization on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

## Directory Structure

Each directory represents a different app containing its respective policy documents:

- **`affirmation/`**: The daily Affirmation app.
  - `privacy.html`: Privacy Policy
  - `terms.html`: Terms of Service
  - `refund.html`: Refund Policy
- **`robon/`**: Robon App.
- **`cibru/`**: Cibru App.
- **`mobile-keyboard/`**: Mobile Keyboard.
- **`universal-video-downloader/`**: Universal Video Downloader.
- **`timer/`**: Timer App.
- **`wa-exported-chat-reader/`**: WA Exported Chat Reader.
- **`torahtone/`**: TorahTone.
- **`cibru-android/`**: Cibru Android client.
- **`larizonai/`**: Larizonai.
