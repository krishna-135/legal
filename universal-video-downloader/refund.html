Refund Policy

Effective Date: August 2, 2026 | Version 1.0

1. Digital Purchases & Premium Upgrades

Global Video Downloader offers Premium features (such as higher concurrent download limits and no bandwidth speed caps) via digital purchases or subscriptions. These transactions are processed directly by the Google Play Store billing system.

2. Refund Eligibility & Google Play Store

Refunds are subject to the policies of the Google Play Store. Users can request a refund for a Premium purchase or subscription within 48 hours of purchase through their Google Play account.

Generally, digital purchases are non-refundable once activated, unless required by local consumer protection laws or if the purchase is found to be defective or not as described.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund from Google Play, follow these steps:

  1. Go to play.google.com/store/account in a web browser.
  2. Select Order History.
  3. Find the purchase transaction for Global Video Downloader Premium.
  4. Click Request a refund or Report a problem, and describe the issue.

If the refund window has closed but you are experiencing technical issues with your Premium purchase, please contact our billing team.

4. Contact Billing Support

For billing-related questions or technical issues regarding Premium upgrades, please email us at billing@globalvideodownloader.app with your transaction details and order ID.
