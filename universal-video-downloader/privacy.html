Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2, 2026 | Version 1.0

Global Video Downloader values your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding information collection, storage, and usage within the application.

1. Information Collection

We process only URLs entered by the user to fetch video metadata and download files directly to your device storage. No user input or downloaded files are transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Local Storage & Retention

Download history and preferences are stored locally using EncryptedSharedPreferences. This data persists on-device until you clear application data or uninstall the application. No data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Open-Source Libraries

This application utilizes open-source components (such as youtubedl-android and FFmpeg) for media processing directly on-device. No tracking, profiling, or analytics SDKs are included in the application.

4. Data Security & Contact

No personal data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties. For privacy inquiries, please contact us at privacy@globalvideodownloader.app.
