Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Thank you for choosing Cibru. This Refund Policy describes the terms regarding transactions, virtual store currencies, and subscriptions within our gamified study application.

1. Simulated Store & Virtual Currencies
All diamond packages, booster items, power-ups, and subscription tiers available in the Cibru store are completely virtual and simulated features. There are no real money transactions processed, and the app does not integrate any real-world payment gateways. Because no real-world money is charged or collected, refund requests for real money are not applicable.

2. Managing Virtual Balances

If you encounter technical issues (such as sync errors, server drops, or local storage crashes) that cause a discrepancy in your mock diamond balance or virtual subscription status, please let us know. We will gladly investigate and manually restore or adjust your progress and virtual balances where appropriate.

3. Future App Store Integrations (Google Play Billing)

In the event that real billing or paid features are integrated into future updates of Cibru, all transactions will be processed securely through the Google Play Store. If that occurs, the following terms will apply:

a) Subscription Cancellation & Management

  • You may cancel any active paid subscription at any time via your Google Play account settings.
  • Upon cancellation, your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.
  • To cancel a subscription:
    1. Open the Google Play Store app on your device.
    2. Tap your profile icon in the top right.
    3. Go to Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions.
    4. Select Cibru and tap Cancel subscription.

b) Google Play Store Refunds

  • Refund requests are governed and processed directly by Google in accordance with the Google Play Refund Policies.
  • Generally, if a refund is requested within 48 hours of purchase, it can be requested directly via your Google Play order history or by visiting play.google.com/store/account.
