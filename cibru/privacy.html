Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cibru ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and your choices regarding your data in our gamified study application.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account Information

When you register and create an account on Cibru, we collect your email address and display name via Google Firebase Authentication to maintain secure authentication and personalize your account profile.

b) Audio Data

The app requests microphone permission to allow you to record and share voice notes in chat threads with other users. This audio data is processed locally and uploaded securely to Firebase Cloud Storage for sharing in chat.

c) App Usage & Progress Data

To provide personalized study feedback, leaderboard features, and a gamified experience, we collect and store:

  • Your study levels, milestones, and badges earned.
  • Your responses and actions in the Test Center.
  • Your mock diamond shop balance.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to facilitate the social chat threads, store study statistics, manage mock diamond shop balances, and synchronize your progress across devices via Firebase.

  • Authentication: To identify you and keep your session secure.
  • Gamified study progress: To compute study statistics and achievements.
  • Social chat features: To deliver chat messages and voice notes in chat threads.
  • Synchronization: To sync data and study state securely across your devices.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Storage) to securely host, synchronize, and protect your study progress and chat messages. These services comply with industry-standard security policies.

4. Data Control & Deletion

Account Deletion and Grace Period: You can request account deletion at any time via the Settings screen in the app. Upon request, your account enters a 2-day grace period during which you can cancel the deletion if you change your mind. After the 2-day period, all your data—including friend lists, chat logs, achievements, and statistics—is permanently deleted from our servers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal details and progress statistics.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated personal data.
  • Revoke permissions (such as microphone access) at any time through your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Cibru is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

7. European Privacy Rights (GDPR) & CCPA

For users in the EU/EEA or California:

  • Legal basis for processing: Consent and contract execution to provide the gamified study features.
  • Data transfers: Data is hosted and processed securely using Google Cloud Firebase services.
  • Data retention: Data is retained as long as your account is active and deleted permanently following account deletion (including the 2-day grace period).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make updates, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy and notify you within the app if appropriate.
