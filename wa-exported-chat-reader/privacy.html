Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2, 2026
Privacy Commitment: Chat Replay operates entirely offline. Your chat files, messages, images, and other media never leave your device.

1. Information We Collect

Chat Replay is designed to respect and protect your privacy. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, chat history, usage logs, or identifiers on any external servers or services. All data processing occurs strictly on your local device.

2. Local Storage and Data Retention

When you choose to import WhatsApp chat exports (.zip or .txt files), the parsed chat messages, images, video, and audio are stored securely within your device's local application sandbox storage. This data is only accessible to Chat Replay and is kept offline. You can delete imported chats at any time from within the app, which immediately purges all associated local records.

3. Security Measures

We leverage standard Android security APIs and filesystem permissions to safeguard the data stored on your device. Because there are no servers hosting your data, there is no risk of cloud-based data breaches or leaks.

4. Third-Party Services

Chat Replay does not integrate with third-party analytical platforms, ad networks, tracking SDKs, or cloud services. In the future, if we integrate any third-party services (such as for crashes or notifications), this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the app is fully offline and does not collect or transmit any data, we do not knowingly request, collect, or store any information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by modifying the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at: support@chatreplay.app
