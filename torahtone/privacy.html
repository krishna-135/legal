Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TorahTone ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. Guest users do not provide any personal information.

b) Location Data

Your location never leaves your device. If you enable the Candle Lighting feature, we request approximate (coarse) location to calculate sunset times locally on your phone. This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

c) Usage Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as:

  • Which features you use (e.g., playing quotes, enabling notifications)
  • App performance metrics (load times, crashes)
  • General device information (OS version, screen size)

This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

d) Subscription Data

If you subscribe to Premium, we store your subscription status, plan type, and subscription identification tokens in our secure database to manage your access. We do not store your payment card details — all payments are securely processed by the Google Play Store billing system via RevenueCat.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: To sign you in and maintain your session
  • Subscription management: To verify your Premium status
  • Notifications: To send daily quote reminders at your local time
  • Sunset calculation: To compute candle lighting times (processed entirely on-device)
  • Analytics: To improve app features and fix bugs

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, analytics, and push notifications
  • Google Play Store / RevenueCat: Subscription and in-app purchase processing

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase's infrastructure, which includes encryption at rest and in transit. User preferences (quote history, notification settings) are stored locally on your device.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data directly from the settings screen in the app or by contacting us
  • Opt out of notifications at any time through Settings
  • Revoke location access through your device's settings

6. Children's Privacy

TorahTone is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

7. Google Play Data Safety

This app's data safety practices comply with Google Play's requirements. For full details, see the Data Safety section in our official app store listing:

  • Google Play Store Listing: TorahTone on Google Play

Data Collection Overview:

  • Data Collected: Name, Email (for Google Sign-In), anonymous usage analytics, subscription status tokens.
  • Data Encryption: All network communication is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS. Databases are encrypted at rest using Google Cloud Firebase security.
  • Location Data: Approximate location is processed strictly on-device for candle lighting sunset calculations and is never uploaded or transmitted to any server.

8. Account & Data Deletion

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Open the TorahTone app.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap "Delete Account" and confirm deletion.

Alternatively, you may submit a deletion request via email to torahtone.support@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". All personal data, Firestore documents, and storage files will be permanently purged within 30 days.

9. European Privacy Rights (GDPR) & CCPA

For users in the EU, UK, or California:

  • Legal Basis for Processing: Contract execution (subscriptions), Legitimate Interest (app analytics & security), and Consent (location access).
  • Right to Portability (GDPR Art. 20): You may request a machine-readable export of your personal data by emailing torahtone.support@gmail.com.
  • Data Retention: Account data is retained while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of deletion request. Analytics data is retained for 14 months.
  • Data Transfers: Data is hosted on Firebase infrastructure located in the United States under standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
