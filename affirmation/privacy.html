Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Affirmation (“the App”) is operated by Affirmation (“we”, “us”, or “our”). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your information. This policy complies with Google Play Store guidelines, relevant data protection laws (including GDPR), and standard guidelines for wellness apps.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: If you sign in (e.g., via Google or email), we collect your provided authentication information (e.g., email address, display name).
  • Usage Analytics: We may collect anonymized usage data (e.g., session time, feature usage) for app improvement via services such as Firebase Analytics or Play Store metrics.
  • Audio Features: Affirmation provides audio playback features. The App does not require, request, or record audio through your device microphone.
  • Subscription and Payment Data: If you upgrade via Google Play Billing, only necessary transaction details are processed by Google and never stored by us directly.
  • Crash/Error Logs: Non-personal diagnostic information may be collected to monitor app reliability (e.g., via Firebase Crashlytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App's core features
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To protect against misuse, fraud, and security threats
  • To communicate with you about updates, offers, and important notices (if you opt in)
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

  • User data is transmitted securely (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Sensitive data (auth tokens, payment info) is encrypted or handled via trusted providers (Firebase, Google)
  • Your data is never shared with third-party marketers
  • Access to sensitive data is strictly limited to authorized personnel only

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We never sell your personal data. Data may be shared only with:

  • Trusted service providers (Firebase, Google) for core app operation
  • Law enforcement or regulatory agencies, but only if required by law

5. Your Rights

  • Access and Correction: Request a copy or correction of information we hold about you
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”), subject to legal requirements
  • Consent: Withdraw app permissions at any time via your device settings

To make a request, contact us at: support@krishna-135.github.io

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted in the app and on this repository.

8. Contact

For privacy or data questions: privacy@krishna-135.github.io

This policy covers all platforms where Affirmation is published (including Google Play).
