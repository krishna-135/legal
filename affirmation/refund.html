Refund Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Thank you for choosing Affirmation.

All subscription purchases and transactions for Affirmation are processed through the Google Play Store.

1. Subscription Management & Cancellation

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time via your Google Play account settings.
  • Upon cancellation, your subscription status will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.
  • Instructions for cancelling via Google Play:
    1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
    2. Tap your profile icon in the top right.
    3. Select Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions.
    4. Select Affirmation and tap Cancel subscription.

2. Refund Eligibility & Request Process

  • Refund requests for subscriptions are managed directly by Google Play in accordance with Google Play Refund Policies.
  • In general:
    • If it has been less than 48 hours since you purchased an app or made an in-app purchase, you can request a refund directly through Google Play.
    • If you purchased a subscription and request a refund within the initial trial or statutory cancellation period, Google Play handles the processing.
  • To request a refund via Google Play:
    1. Go to play.google.com/store/account.
    2. Click Order History and locate your Affirmation transaction.
    3. Select Request a refund or Report a problem and complete the form.

3. Contact Us

If you experience any technical issues with your subscription, please contact our support team at support@krishna-135.github.io.
